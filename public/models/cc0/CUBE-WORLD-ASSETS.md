# Cube World Kit fauna

These three animal models are from Quaternius' official Cube World Kit:

- `mc-wolf.gltf`
- `mc-raccoon.gltf`
- `mc-sheep.gltf`

Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/cubeworldkit.html

The pack is released under CC0 and permits personal and commercial use. The
selected glTF files are self-contained and include embedded mesh data, material
data and animation clips. They are loaded from `src/model-assets.ts` and used by the
runtime fauna system in `src/entities.ts`.
