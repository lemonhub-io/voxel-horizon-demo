# Gobkit animal models

These models were downloaded from Gobkit's free animal pack on 2026-07-31.
The pack is published under CC0 1.0 and may be modified, bundled, and
redistributed in commercial or non-commercial projects.

| File | Source | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| `gobkit-rhino.glb` | https://gobkit.com/freebies/animal/Rhino.glb | terrestrial quadruped |
| `gobkit-platypus.glb` | https://gobkit.com/freebies/animal/Platypus.glb | terrestrial alien-like creature |
| `gobkit-anglerfish.glb` | https://gobkit.com/freebies/animal/Anglerfish.glb | aquatic or special habitat creature |

The models are rigged low-poly GLB assets with idle, attack, dead, and walk
animation ranges. They are authored facing +Z with +Y up. Production builds
should bundle the local files rather than hotlinking the source URLs.

License and manifest: https://gobkit.com/api/free
